## What was built

A typed-record registry subpackage, `orbital_thermal.registry`, that gives every
load-bearing Phase B property and correlation an explicit **provenance class** and a
**resolution status**, and enforces one invariant: **a non-rank-eligible entry cannot
silently enter a ranked Phase B case.** Unresolved items are registered with a
machine-visible blocker status and no value -- never invented.

Files (7 new):

- `src/orbital_thermal/registry/provenance.py` -- framework: `Provenance`/`Status`
  enums, `Source`, `Domain` (validity ranges), `PropertyEntry`, `CorrelationEntry`,
  the `rank_eligible` rule, and the guards `assert_rank_eligible` / `assert_in_domain`
  (`NotRankEligibleError`).
- `src/orbital_thermal/registry/properties.py` -- coolant, solid, and containment
  entries.
- `src/orbital_thermal/registry/correlations.py` -- thermal and hydraulic correlations
  (executable closed forms where they are one-liners).
- `src/orbital_thermal/registry/__init__.py` -- public API (`get`, `ALL_ENTRIES`,
  `rank_eligible_entries`, `blocked_entries`, `summary`).
- `tests/test_registry.py` -- 41 tests.
- `scripts/generate_property_registry.py` -- renders the registry to the doc.
- `docs/property-provenance.md` -- generated provenance table (single source of truth
  is the code).

The registry is numpy/stdlib-only and **imports without CoolProp** (matching the
package convention). Resolved coolant transport values are **derived** from CoolProp
7.2.0 at the saturated-liquid 300 K reference state and stored as pinned literals; the
test suite re-derives them (skipping when CoolProp is absent).

## Registry surface

39 entries: **19 rank-eligible, 20 blocked** (resolved=27, source_required=9,
sensitivity=1, future=2). *(Counts reflect the spot-check follow-up below: the 8 coolant
transport literals are `reference_anchor` kind -- resolved and value-carrying, but not
rank-eligible -- and are replaced for ranked use by 2 `backend_evaluation` entries.)*

**Rank-eligible:** the ammonia/water **`property_backend`** per-state evaluation entries
(the rank-eligible path to coolant properties); ammonia critical temperature and CO2
critical point (PUBLISHED anchors); aluminium and copper conductivity (PUBLISHED,
Incropera); Al-6061-T6 / Ti-6Al-4V density and Al-6061-T6 yield (PUBLISHED); friction
(laminar / Blasius / Haaland), Nusselt (laminar 4.36 / 3.66, Dittus-Boelter,
Gnielinski), spreading-resistance model (sourced), and the minor-loss K-factor method.

**Reference anchors (not rank-eligible):** ammonia and water density / specific heat /
thermal conductivity / viscosity -- single 300 K saturated-liquid literals DERIVED from
CoolProp 7.2.0, kept for cross-check only. A ranked case must use the `property_backend`
entry for per-state values, enforced by `assert_rank_eligible`.

## Scope boundaries (what B1 does NOT do)

Per the B1 scope: no coupled B2-B4 solver; no architecture ranking; no Biswas/Suncatcher
reproduction; no Dash; no invented values; **CO2 is not rank-eligible in Stage 1.** B0.5
(the Phase A visual notebook) was not touched.

## Limitations and readiness

- The 12 blocked entries are B1's declared, machine-visible gaps; each is resolved in a
  later step (B1 follow-up sourcing, B2/B3 modelling) and is *provably* excluded from any
  ranked case by `assert_rank_eligible`.
- Representative coolant values are single reference-state numbers; per-state property
  evaluation over the loop is B3 (via `orbital_thermal.fluids`).
- Executable forms for spreading resistance and the minor-loss inventory land in B2/B3;
  their registry entries fix the source and domain now.
- **Readiness for (optional) cross-model review:** the highest-value checks are
  (a) are the resolved values and their sources correct; (b) is any entry wrongly marked
  rank-eligible; (c) does the enforcement actually block silent use of a blocked entry.
  All three are covered by tests.

## Spot-check follow-up (cross-model, 2026-07-02)

A scoped cross-model spot-check (GPT-5.5) reviewed the resolved values/sources, the
rank-eligibility classifications, and the enforcement. It found **no blocker** and cleared
the physical constants, the CoolProp magnitudes, the correlation math, and the CO2 / PGW /
containment classifications. Two findings were raised and both are addressed here:

| # | Sev | Disposition | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | major | **fixed** | The single-state coolant literals were rank-eligible over a broad domain, so a ranked case could use a 300 K reference number as an operational loop property. Added `PropertyKind`: those literals are now `reference_anchor` (resolved, value kept for cross-check, **not** rank-eligible), and 2 `backend_evaluation` entries (`coolant.{ammonia,water}.property_backend`) are the rank-eligible per-state path. Enforced and tested. |
| F2 | minor | **fixed** | The explicit blocked-entry test covered only a subset. Replaced with an exhaustive `test_every_blocked_entry_raises` parametrized over `registry.blocked_entries()`. |

The verified value/source and correlation checks from the spot-check are recorded above; the
registry is sound for B2 to build on.
