## Physical question
How is reflected-solar (albedo) load on the radiator modelled as a function of orbital beta
angle, and what is the known limitation of the sub-point albedo formulation at beta = 90 deg?

## Why it matters
Albedo is part of the short-wave load feeding the effective sink. Phase B reuses this model
for reported (orbital-boundary) trade spaces, so its **documented limitation must travel with
it**: the sub-point albedo factor nulls at beta = 90 deg, which is a known model-form
limitation, not a physical claim that albedo is zero there. A corrective fix (Phase A
PR #24) enforced no-invention behavior at this boundary; that history is part of this entry.

## Governing relation and variable definitions
Albedo load uses a sub-point (sub-solar) geometric factor that depends on beta and orbital
position:

```
q_albedo = albedo * q_solar * f_albedo(beta, position) * F_view
f_albedo(beta = 90 deg) -> 0     (documented model limitation)
```

- `beta` orbit-to-sun beta angle [deg]; `albedo` planetary albedo [-]
- `f_albedo` sub-point geometric factor [-]; `F_view` Earth view factor [-] (see
  `earth-view-factors`)

## Assumptions
Sub-point (sub-solar) albedo approximation; diffuse planetary reflection; the geometric
factor is a reduced-order stand-in for a full spherical-integration of reflected flux.

## Sensitivity / limiting cases
- `beta = 0` (sun in orbit plane) vs `beta = 90` (sun normal to orbit plane) bound the albedo
  contribution; the `beta = 90` null is the documented limitation.
- Reported trade spaces must surface this limitation rather than treating the null as physical.

## Known uncertainties
The sub-point approximation under-represents off-sub-point reflected flux; near beta = 90 the
model is least trustworthy. This narrows applicability; it is not a license to invent a value.

## What evidence would invalidate this result
- A full reflected-flux integration showing materially non-zero albedo at beta = 90 that
  changes a ranking.
- Evidence the sub-point approximation is inadequate across the intended screening range.
